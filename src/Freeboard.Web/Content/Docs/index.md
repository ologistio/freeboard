# Introduction

This documentation is being written. The pages below hold placeholder text until the
matching capability ships.

## How these pages are organised

The rail on the left lists every page in reading order, grouped by subject. Guides are
prose with worked examples; the legal group holds the agreements that govern use of
Freeboard.

## Adding a page

Add a markdown file under `Content/Docs`, then list it in `Content/Docs/docs.json` with
its rail label, page title and lede. The body starts at a level-two heading; the title
comes from the manifest, and level-two headings become the contents list on the right.
